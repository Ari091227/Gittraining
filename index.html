14-Day Journey

Tokyo • Kyoto • Osaka
Day 1 Arrival

Tokyo Arrival

Tokyo
  • Arrive in Tokyo and settle into a central hotel in Shinjuku or Ueno.
  • Easy evening walk around Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko.
  • Keep the first night relaxed with ramen and city lights.
Day 2 Culture

Harajuku & Meiji

Tokyo
  • Visit Meiji Shrine, Omotesando, and Harajuku streets.
  • Spend late afternoon in Shibuya Sky or Tokyo Tower.
  • Enjoy a relaxed food crawl with sushi and desserts.
Day 3 Heritage

Asakusa & TeamLab

Tokyo
  • Explore Senso-ji Temple and the historic lanes of Asakusa.
  • Visit Tokyo Skytree and Sumida River area.
  • Evening optional stop in Akihabara or Tokyo Station.
Day 4 Day trip

Mt. Fuji Route

Tokyo
  • Choose a Fuji Five Lakes or Hakone day trip if weather is clear.
  • Optionally keep it local with Odaiba and waterfront views.
  • Use the day to recharge before heading farther south.
Day 5 City

Imperial Tokyo

Tokyo
  • Walk the Imperial Palace East Gardens and surrounding neighborhoods.
  • Visit Ginza for shopping, cafés, and premium city energy.
  • Finish the night with a ramen crawl or izakaya dinner.
Day 6 Transit

Tokyo to Kyoto

Kyoto
  • Take the Shinkansen early morning to Kyoto.
  • Check in and explore Gion, Yasaka Shrine, and Kamo River.
  • Evening stroll in the lantern-lit alleys of Higashiyama.
Day 7 Temples

Kyoto Heritage

Kyoto
  • Visit Kiyomizu-dera, Kodai-ji, and the historic lanes of Ninenzaka.
  • Take a slow afternoon around Kiyamachi and Pontocho.
  • Try Kyoto-style kaiseki or noodle specialties.
Day 8 Scenic

Arashiyama

Kyoto
  • Walk through the bamboo grove and Tenryu-ji Temple.
  • Enjoy riverside views and the iconic Togetsukyo Bridge.
  • Optional monkey park stop for added adventure.
Day 9 Sacred

Fushimi Inari

Kyoto
  • Begin early with a sunrise climb at Fushimi Inari Taisha.
  • Visit Nishiki Market for snacks, sweets, and local foods.
  • Spend the late afternoon at a tea house or café.
Day 10 City

Kyoto to Osaka

Osaka
  • Transfer to Osaka and stretch out in Namba.
  • Explore Dotonbori and soak in the neon food streets.
  • Evening at Umeda Sky Building or a local bar alley.
Day 11 Fun

Osaka Icons

Osaka
  • Choose between Universal Studios Japan or Osaka Castle.
  • Enjoy the evening food crawl and shopping in Dotonbori.
  • Use the stop to enjoy Osaka’s energetic nightlife.
Day 12 Day trip

Nara Escape

Nara
  • Visit Nara Park and see the famous free-roaming deer.
  • Explore Todai-ji Temple and Kasuga Taisha.
  • Relax with a light local lunch and scenic walk.
Day 13 Flex

Free Day

Flexible
  • Optional trip to Hiroshima and Miyajima if you want more history.
  • Or keep the day for shopping, cafés, and rest in Osaka or Kyoto.
  • Use flexibility to absorb delays or a slower travel rhythm.
Day 14 Departure

Departure Day

Airport
  • Do a final morning walk or local shopping in Osaka/Kyoto.
  • Head to KIX or the nearest airport for your return flight.
  • Keep the final day light and easy for a stress-free exit.

Travel Tips

Smart planning
  • Book the Tokyo–Kyoto Shinkansen early to lock in better fares and smoother schedules.
  • Choose a hotel near a train station or metro line to cut daily commutes and save money.
  • Carry a Suica or IC card for easy public transport payments across major cities.
  • Keep one flexible day free for weather, travel delays, or spontaneous side trips.
  • Prioritize food streets like Dotonbori, Nishiki Market, and ramen hubs to get the most value from your budget.
